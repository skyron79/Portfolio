PROJECT 05

Inclusive Hair Platform

A platform to help people with textured hair discover, save and book trusted stylists in Flanders.

group
View case study →
ROLE
YEAR 2026

THE CASE STUDY

From challenge to impact.

01

The Challenge

Finding trusted afro-hair stylists in Flanders is difficult. Many work from home, which makes discovering and booking them more challenging.

02

The Solution

A Pinterest-like discovery experience where users can save hairstyles, find stylists, explore portfolios and connect in one place.

03

The Impact

More visibility for stylists, easier access for clients, and a stronger, more inclusive textured-hair community in Flanders.
